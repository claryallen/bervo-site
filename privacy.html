Bervo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 (version 1.2). Version 1.0 was effective September 13, 2026, and version 1.1 on September 16, 2026. Version 1.2 corrects how you sign in and what we read from your contacts; states that Bervo only reads your Google and Microsoft accounts; adds beta feedback and texts with Bervo; describes the Mac app's calendar and contacts sync; states retention periods precisely; and explains deletion by request during the private beta.

1. Who we are

Bervo LLC ("Bervo", "we") is a Florida limited liability company. Bervo provides a private, AI-based executive assistant that reads the email, calendar, contacts, and, where you choose, text messages you connect to it, and produces briefings, reminders, and a searchable record of your commitments and relationships, with an AI layer that summarizes and organizes it and an assistant you direct in chat. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, who can see it, how you control what the assistant sees, and how you delete it.

2. The short version

  • Your data lives on your own private server (your "instance"). It is never pooled with other customers' data.
  • You reach your dashboard with a one-time code we email you, then your Bervo password. Google and Microsoft sign-in is used only to connect your mail, calendar, and contacts, with permissions you grant there; the Bervo Mac app, if you install it, syncs from your Mac. You can revoke either at any time, from your Google or Microsoft account or by removing the Mac app.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models — and neither does the AI provider.
  • AI processing happens through your own API key with the model provider, under your account with that provider, or through a Bervo-managed key during a trial.
  • During the private beta, deletion is by request: email privacy@bervo.ai. We destroy your server and its backups within 7 days and confirm completion in writing.
  • Feedback you send reaches the Bervo team. Separately, and only if you agree, the team may review your conversations with Bervo during the beta to fix problems. You can say no, or change your mind at any time (§11).

Setup answers. The choices you make during setup, such as which accounts to connect, how much history to load, and your privacy and notification settings, are kept on your own server.

4. Where your data lives and who can reach it

  • Your instance. Your data is stored and processed on your own private server, which we provision for you at DigitalOcean in the United States. Your files, databases, scheduled jobs, and keys are yours alone and are never pooled with another customer's. If we later offer a lower-priced plan in which your instance is a sealed space of its own on a server shared with other customers — still with its own files, databases, jobs, and keys, and no shared database — we will describe it here and tell you before you choose it.
  • Your devices (optional). If you install the Bervo Mac app, it securely syncs your Messages and, if you choose, the mail in Apple Mail, Apple Calendar, and Contacts to your private server. It needs macOS Full Disk Access to read Messages. It syncs while your Mac is awake and catches up when it wakes. Photos and other attachments in your texts are never copied; a photo appears only as "[Photo shared]". Texts with a contact you exclude are deleted by your server the moment they arrive, before anything reads them. Your other privacy choices (§5) are applied on your server. On your Mac, the app keeps only its own settings, its pairing token, and your exclusion list. The Mac app reads nothing else on your Mac and can be uninstalled at any time.
  • Custodianship. Because we operate the server, the Bervo team can technically reach it. Access is limited to the Bervo team and to automated health checks that hold no data-reading rights. It is used only to operate the service — to keep your instance running or to carry out a request you make — and it is logged. We do not browse customer data. The only other access is the beta conversation review in §11, and only if you agree to it.
  • Sub-processors. We use the following providers to run the service: DigitalOcean (hosting); Anthropic (the AI model, under your key or, during a trial, ours); Photon (delivery of texts between you and Bervo's number); Cloudflare (our domain, the sign-in gateway that emails your one-time code, and the encrypted tunnel in front of your server); Tailscale (Bervo's private operations network); Microsoft and Google (the accounts you connect). Each receives only what its role requires.

5. Your privacy choices

Sanitization is our word for the masking you choose to apply before any of your data (email, calendar, contacts, or messages) reaches the AI model or is stored in summaries: last names and email addresses. You set the level; each level costs the assistant some precision.

You set these during onboarding and can change them at any time. Each choice is explained with a concrete example of what the assistant sees with and without it, and with its cost, because every exclusion makes the assistant less useful in a specific way.

  • Name and address handling. Two separate switches, both off by default: hide last names (the assistant sees "Timothy E" instead of "Timothy Edwards"), and hide email addresses. Hiding happens on your private server before anything reaches the AI. Your originals stay on your server, which is what lets you change your mind later, in either direction.
  • Attachments. Bervo does not download or open attachments; it only sees that one is there.
  • Text messages. Photos, videos, and other attachments in your texts are never copied; a photo appears only as "[Photo shared]". You can exclude a contact: your server deletes their texts the moment they arrive, before anything reads them. We encourage including everyone, because family threads often carry the plans and reminders Bervo is best at.
  • Assistant name. You may rename the assistant; the name is stored as a setting and shown wherever the assistant addresses you.

6. People you add to your instance

You may invite people to work in your instance with you, such as an assistant or a colleague. For each person we collect their name, email address, and login, and we record which of them did what, so actions in your instance are attributed to the person who took them. People you add can see the data in your instance; they are users of your instance, not sources of data, and Bervo never reads their own email, calendar, or messages. You are responsible for adding only people who are permitted to see the data you have connected, including under any obligation you have to your employer or clients, and for removing them when that permission ends. You can remove a person at any time from Settings, and their access ends immediately.

7. Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

Bervo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google Workspace User Data and Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we request. When you connect a Google account we ask only to read your Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts. Bervo never sends, changes, or deletes mail, events, or contacts.

How we use it. We use Google user data only to provide the assistant features you see in Bervo. We do not transfer it except to provide those features (including to the AI provider as described in §9), for security purposes, to comply with law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to you. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models; the only learning is your own instance's, for you alone (§9). Humans do not read it except with your explicit consent (for example, the beta conversation review in §11, if you agree to it, or when you ask us to look at something specific), when necessary for security, to comply with law, or in aggregated, anonymized form for internal operations.

8. Microsoft account data

When you connect a Microsoft account, we request delegated permissions to read mail, calendars, and contacts. We request no permission to write or send mail. Your organization's administrator may need to approve Bervo for your tenant; that approval covers only the permissions listed on the consent screen.

9. AI processing

Summaries and answers are produced by sending the relevant text to a large-language-model provider (Anthropic) over an API. Where you supply your own API key, those requests are made under your account with that provider and are governed by its terms. Where Bervo supplies the key during a trial, requests are made under Bervo's account and the provider's API terms, which do not permit training on API inputs. We apply your privacy choices (§5) on your server before anything is sent to the provider: text exclusions and name and address hiding. Search indexing uses a model that runs on your own server, so it sends nothing to the provider. Nothing excluded is sent to the provider.

What learns, and what doesn't. Your Bervo instance learns from your data and your corrections: how you write, who matters to you, what you want to be told about, and what you've committed to. That learning stays on your server, is never used for another customer, and is destroyed when you delete your instance. The AI model provider does not learn from you: requests are sent under API terms that do not permit training on them, and no data of yours is used to train any model, by us or by anyone.

10. Questions during setup

Setup screens have a Questions box. What you type there goes to the Bervo team, and a person replies. It is kept with your other feedback (§11).

11. Beta feedback and conversation review

Feedback you send. During the beta you can send feedback to the Bervo team in three ways: by text (just tell Bervo something is wrong, confusing, or could be better), with the Feedback button, or by ticking "Also send to the Bervo team" on a comment. Feedback you write to us yourself (the Feedback button, or the Questions box during setup) reaches the Bervo team as you wrote it, with your name and address hiding choices (§5) applied, plus basic context (which screen, how many messages) but never your conversations. When you tell Bervo something went wrong by text or chat, Bervo sends the team only a general description of the problem (which feature, what went wrong), never the content of your messages, data, or tasks; the details stay on your server. The Bervo team keeps it outside your instance, on Bervo's message service, until you ask us to delete it, and may be alerted to a problem report by text.

Conversation review — only with your consent. During the beta, if you agree, the Bervo team may review your conversations with Bervo — your chats and texts with the assistant, not your email or your texts with other people — to find and fix problems. This is done by an automatic nightly check on your own server, using your own AI key, which sends the team only a general description of each problem it finds (which feature, what went wrong), never the exchange itself or any of your content.

This consent is separate from agreeing to these terms. It is off unless you choose it, you can decline it and still use Bervo, and you can withdraw it at any time by texting Bervo or emailing privacy@bervo.ai, and a team member switches it off. It ends when the beta ends.

12. Retention and deletion

  • While you are a customer, your data is kept on your instance so the assistant can reason over history:
  • the full text of messages you receive: a rolling 90 days;
  • the full text of mail you send: for as long as your account exists;
  • summaries, and everything extracted from your messages (tasks, deadlines, commitments, relationships), plus your notes and conversations with Bervo: for as long as your account exists, however far back you chose to load.

Outside your instance, feedback and setup questions are kept as described in §11. - When you leave: during the private beta, deletion is by request: email privacy@bervo.ai, and a person handles it. We confirm the request is yours, then destroy your server and its backups within 7 days, and we confirm completion in writing. What we hold about you outside your server is deleted at the same time: your feedback and setup questions, and your phone number's link to Bervo's text line. Once paid plans exist, if your subscription ends without a deletion request, we do the same 30 days after it ends. - Your source data is never changed. Bervo only reads. Nothing Bervo does modifies or deletes anything in your email, calendar, contacts, or messages; they remain yours, at their source, before, during, and after you use Bervo. Deleting your Bervo instance deletes Bervo's copies and what Bervo generated, nothing else. - Export. Everything Bervo generates for you — tasks, notes, initiatives, summaries, and your conversations with the assistant — is yours. Ask us and we will send you a copy of your server's databases and your notes, and we offer it before any deletion. - Revocation without deletion: removing Bervo's access in your Google or Microsoft account, removing your Mac in Settings → Devices, or uninstalling the Mac app stops new data immediately; existing data remains on your instance until you ask us to delete it or you leave.

13. Security

Your server has no open ports to the internet. Traffic reaches it only through an encrypted tunnel, after you enter a one-time code we email to your sign-in address, and your Bervo password sits on top of that. Credentials to your connected accounts are stored as revocable tokens on your instance only, never in a shared store, and so is your AI key. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you without undue delay and within any period required by law.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to correct or restrict your personal information, and to object to certain processing; we honor those too. Contact privacy@bervo.ai.
