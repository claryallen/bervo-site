Bervo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026 (version 1.0).

1. Who we are

Bervo LLC ("Bervo", "we") is a Florida limited liability company. Bervo provides a private, AI-based executive assistant that reads the email, calendar, contacts, and, where you choose, text messages you connect to it, and produces briefings, reminders, drafts, and a searchable record of your commitments and relationships, with an AI layer that summarizes and organizes it and an assistant you direct in chat. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, who can see it, how you control what the assistant sees, and how you delete it.

2. The short version

  • Your data runs on a server dedicated to you (your "instance"). It is not pooled with other customers' data.
  • We access your accounts only with permissions you grant through Google or Microsoft sign-in, or through software you install on your own device. You can revoke either at any time from your Google or Microsoft account, or by uninstalling the software.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models — and neither does the AI provider.
  • AI processing happens through your own API key with the model provider, under your account with that provider, or through a Bervo-managed key during a trial.
  • You can delete everything yourself from Settings. Deletion starts immediately; your server and its backups are destroyed within 7 days, and we confirm completion in writing.

4. Where your data lives and who can reach it

  • Your instance. Each customer's data is stored and processed on a dedicated server we provision for that customer at DigitalOcean, in the United States. Nothing about your instance is shared with another customer's.
  • Hosted Mac (optional). If you choose the hosted text-message option, you get your own dedicated account on a Mac we operate, on Apple hardware in a data center in the United States, signed into your Apple ID with only Messages in iCloud enabled. We never enable iCloud Drive, Photos, Keychain, or any other iCloud service for it. You sign in yourself; we never see or store your Apple ID password. For your SMS and RCS conversations to reach Bervo, you turn on Text Message Forwarding to that Mac on your iPhone. You can remove that Mac from your Apple ID at any time from your iPhone (Settings → your name → Devices), which ends its access immediately.
  • Your devices (optional). If you install the Bervo Mac app, it reads the messages and, if you choose, the mail already on your Mac and sends them to your server; your exclusions (§5) are applied on the Mac before anything is sent. If you install the Bervo Android app, it reads your SMS and MMS messages with the permission you grant, sees incoming RCS messages through notifications only, and cannot read outgoing RCS messages on current phones; the same exclusions are applied on the phone before anything is sent. Neither app reads anything else on the device, and both can be uninstalled at any time.
  • Custodianship. Because we operate the server, our personnel can technically reach it. Access is limited to the Bervo team and to automated health checks that hold no data-reading rights, is used only to keep your instance running or to carry out a request you make, and is logged. We do not browse customer data.
  • Sub-processors. We use the following providers to run the service: DigitalOcean (hosting); Anthropic (the AI model, under your key or ours); Photon (text-message delivery for the assistant's own number); Cloudflare (our domain, and the encrypted tunnel and sign-in gateway in front of your server); Tailscale (Bervo's private operations network); Microsoft and Google (the accounts you connect); Apple (Messages in iCloud, for the hosted-Mac option). Each receives only what its role requires.

5. Your privacy choices

Sanitization is our word for the masking you choose to apply before any of your data (email, calendar, contacts, or messages) reaches the AI model or is stored in summaries: last names and email addresses. You set the level; each level costs the assistant some precision.

You set these during onboarding and can change them at any time. Each choice is explained with a concrete example of what the assistant sees with and without it, and with its cost, because every exclusion makes the assistant less useful in a specific way.

  • Name and address handling. Choose whether the assistant sees last names and email addresses, or only first name and last initial.
  • Attachments are not downloaded or processed.
  • Text messages. Photos, videos, and other attachments in your texts are never captured or stored. Per contact, choose include, filter, or exclude. Exclude means that contact's messages never leave your device. Filter keeps the contact but removes individual messages that are strictly personal with no task, commitment, or scheduling content. That sorting is done by a small model running on your own server, so filtered messages are never sent to the AI provider and are not stored.
  • Assistant name. You may rename the assistant; the name is stored as a setting and shown wherever the assistant addresses you.

6. People you add to your instance

You may invite people to work in your instance with you, such as an assistant or a colleague. For each person we collect their name, email address, and login, and we record which of them did what, so actions in your instance are attributed to the person who took them. People you add can see the data in your instance; they are users of your instance, not sources of data, and Bervo never reads their own email, calendar, or messages. You are responsible for adding only people who are permitted to see the data you have connected, including under any obligation you have to your employer or clients, and for removing them when that permission ends. You can remove a person at any time from Settings, and their access ends immediately.

7. Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

Bervo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: we use Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts data only to provide the assistant features you see in Bervo; we do not transfer it except to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you; we do not use it for advertising; and humans do not read it except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or in aggregated, anonymized form for internal operations.

8. Microsoft account data

When you sign in with Microsoft, we request delegated permissions to read mail, calendars, and contacts, and, for the drafting feature, to write draft messages to your mailbox. We never request permission to send mail unless you or your organization's administrator explicitly enable a send feature. Your organization's administrator may need to approve Bervo for your tenant; that approval covers only the permissions listed on the consent screen.

9. AI processing

Summaries, drafts, and answers are produced by sending the relevant text to a large-language-model provider (Anthropic) over an API. Where you supply your own API key, those requests are made under your account with that provider and are governed by its terms. Where Bervo supplies the key during a trial, requests are made under Bervo's account and the provider's API terms, which do not permit training on API inputs. We apply your privacy choices (§5) before anything is sent to the provider: exclusions on your device, the text-message relevance filter and search indexing with models that run on your own server, and name and address masking on your server. Nothing a filter removes is sent to the provider.

What learns, and what doesn't. Your Bervo instance learns from your data and your corrections: how you write, who matters to you, what you want to be told about, and what you've committed to. That learning stays on your server, is never used for another customer, and is destroyed when you delete your instance. The AI model provider does not learn from you: requests are sent under API terms that do not permit training on them, and no data of yours is used to train any model, by us or by anyone.

10. Retention and deletion

  • While you are a customer: connected-account data, generated summaries, and your notes are retained on your instance so the assistant can reason over history. Full message bodies are kept on your server for a rolling 90-day window; summaries and everything extracted from them are kept for the life of your account, however far back you chose to load. Older full text is fetched again from your own mailbox when you ask to see it.
  • When you leave: you can delete everything yourself from Settings at any time. Deletion starts immediately; we destroy your server and its backups within 7 days, and we confirm completion in writing. If your subscription ends without a deletion request, we do the same 30 days after it ends. Deletion is one operation, run and logged.
  • Your source data is never changed. Bervo only reads. Nothing Bervo does modifies or deletes anything in your email, calendar, contacts, or messages; they remain yours, at their source, before, during, and after you use Bervo. Deleting your Bervo instance deletes Bervo's copies and what Bervo generated, nothing else.
  • Export at any time. Everything Bervo generates for you — tasks, notes, initiatives, summaries, and your conversations with the assistant — can be downloaded from Settings at any time, in a portable form (CSV and Markdown, plus the raw database files if you want them), and is provided to you before deletion.
  • Revocation without deletion: removing Bervo's access in your Google or Microsoft account, or uninstalling the device software, stops new data immediately; existing data remains on your instance until you delete it or leave.

11. Security

Your server has no open ports to the internet. Traffic reaches it only through an encrypted tunnel after you sign in with your existing Google or Microsoft account, and your Bervo login sits on top of that. Credentials to your connected accounts are stored as revocable OAuth tokens on your instance only, never in a shared store. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you without undue delay and within any period required by law.

12. Age and business use

Bervo is for business use by adults. We do not verify anyone's age. You are responsible for who you allow to use your instance.

13. Your rights

Bervo is a small US company. We honor access, export, and deletion requests from anyone, regardless of where you live, because your data sits on a dedicated server we can hand you or destroy. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to correct or restrict your personal information, and to object to certain processing; we honor those too. Contact privacy@bervo.ai.
