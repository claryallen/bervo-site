Bervo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 (version 1.18). Earlier versions are listed under Version history in section 16.

1. Who we are

Bervo LLC ("Bervo", "we") is a Florida limited liability company. Bervo provides a private, AI-based executive assistant that reads the email, calendar, contacts, and, where you choose, text messages you connect to it, and produces briefings, reminders, and a searchable record of your commitments and relationships, with an AI layer that summarizes and organizes it and an assistant you direct in chat. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, who can see it, how you control what the assistant sees, and how you delete it.

2. The short version

  • Your data lives in your own private space on a Bervo server (your “instance”). On the Dedicated plan, your instance is a private server of its own. On the Standard plan, it is your own walled-off account on a shared Bervo server, with its own files, databases, scheduled jobs and keys, and no database shared with any other customer. On either plan, your data is never pooled with other customers' data.
  • You reach your dashboard with a one-time code we email you, then your Bervo password. Google and Microsoft sign-in is used only to connect your mail, calendar, and contacts, with permissions you grant there; the Bervo Mac app, if you install it, syncs from your Mac. You can revoke either at any time, from your Google or Microsoft account or by removing the Mac app.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models — and neither does the AI provider.
  • AI processing happens through your own API key with the model provider, under your account with that provider, or through a Bervo-managed key during a trial.
  • During the private beta, deletion is by request: email privacy@bervo.ai. We remove your instance within 2 days of your request (on Dedicated, by destroying your server; on Standard, by removing your account, its files and its backups from the shared server), remaining backup copies expire within 7 days after that, and we confirm completion in writing.
  • Feedback you send reaches the Bervo team. Separately, and only if you agree, the team may review your conversations with Bervo during the beta to fix problems. You can say no, or change your mind at any time (§11).

3. What we collect

Account information. Your name; the email address you sign in with, where we send your one-time sign-in codes; your mobile number, if you give it (see "Your texts with Bervo" below); your home time zone; your Bervo address (for example, yourname.bervo.ai); and your settings, including what you choose to exclude (§5).

Setup answers. The choices you make during setup, such as which accounts to connect and your privacy and notification settings, are kept in your own instance.

Invite requests. When you ask for an invite on bervo.ai, we keep what you enter: your name, email address, whether you have an assistant, which email services, computer(s) and phone you use, the plan you’re interested in, whether you said you’re ready to set up and start using Bervo within a few days, how you heard about Bervo, and what you tell us about yourself, and whether you came through a personal link we shared. We also note your time zone from your browser and when you agreed to this policy. To stop spam, we keep a scrambled code made from your internet address for 7 days, never the address itself. We use all this only to review your request and set up your account, never for marketing. When we email you about your invite, we keep a record of each email (a scrambled form of your address and whether it was delivered), never its text. If we don’t invite you, if we haven’t answered, or if an invitation goes unused, we delete the request and those records 90 days later. If you become a customer, we keep only a reference number for your request, your email address, your first name and last initial, the web address of your Bervo server (for example, k7m2qx.bervo.ai), your plan, and the date you requested your invite and agreed to this policy. When we email you about your Bervo account, such as to say your account is loaded or to help you add your texts, we keep a record of each email (which email it was, a scrambled form of your address, the web address of your Bervo server, whether it was delivered, and when), never its text, and we delete each record 90 days after the email is sent.

4. Where your data lives and who can reach it

  • Your instance. Your data is stored and processed in your own instance, at DigitalOcean in the United States. Bervo has two plans, and they differ only in where your instance runs:
    • Dedicated. Your instance is a private server of its own, which we provision for you.
    • Standard. Your instance is your own walled-off account on a server shared with other Bervo customers. Your account has its own files, databases, scheduled jobs and keys, separate from every other customer’s; there is no shared database. Other customers on the server cannot read your files or reach your instance. Your instance’s nightly backups are kept on that server, readable only by your account and the server’s administrators, separately from every other customer’s.
    On either plan your files, databases, scheduled jobs, and keys are yours alone and are never pooled with another customer’s, and DigitalOcean also keeps daily backups of the whole server.
  • Your devices (optional). If you install the Bervo Mac app, it securely syncs your Messages and, if you choose, the mail in Apple Mail, Apple Calendar, and Contacts to your instance. It needs macOS Full Disk Access to read Messages. It syncs while your Mac is awake and catches up when it wakes. Photos and other attachments in your texts are never copied; a photo appears only as "[Photo shared]". Texts with a contact you exclude are deleted by your instance the moment they arrive, before anything reads them. Your other privacy choices (§5) are applied in your instance. On your Mac, the app keeps only its own settings, its pairing token, and your exclusion list. The Mac app reads nothing else on your Mac and can be uninstalled at any time.
  • The Bervo app for Android (optional). If you install the Bervo app on your Android phone, it reads the text of your SMS and MMS messages (sent and received) from your phone's message store. It also reads the text, sender and time of message notifications from Google Messages and Samsung Messages, so it can include messages that arrive in encrypted chats. It reads your phone's contacts only to match names and numbers for your messages. It never reads photos, videos or other attachments: for each attachment it notes only that one exists and its file type and name. It does not read drafts or unsent messages, and it never sends, changes or deletes a message. What it reads goes only to your own Bervo server, over an encrypted connection, about every 15 minutes.
  • Custodianship. Because we operate the server, the Bervo team can technically reach it. Access is limited to the Bervo team and to automated health checks that hold no data-reading rights. It is used only to operate the service — to keep your instance running or to carry out a request you make — and it is logged. We do not browse customer data. The only other access is the automatic nightly check of the Beta Conversation Improvement Program in §11, which runs in your own instance and sends us no content, and only if you join it.
  • Sub-processors. We use the following providers to run the service: DigitalOcean (hosting); Anthropic (the AI model, under your key or, during a trial, ours); Photon (delivery of texts between you and Bervo's number); Cloudflare (our domain, the sign-in gateway that emails your one-time code, the encrypted tunnel in front of your instance, and the invite request form, including its spam check); Postmark (sending account emails such as your invite, setup reminders and the notice that your account is ready; it receives your name and email address and keeps a copy of each message for 7 days); Tailscale (Bervo's private operations network); Microsoft and Google (the accounts you connect); Deepgram (speech-to-text and text-to-speech for Voice, only for people who turn Voice on). Each receives only what its role requires.

5. Masking and your privacy choices

Before any of your data (email, calendar, contacts or messages) reaches the AI, your instance cuts the last names of the people Bervo knows about (your contacts and the people you email and text with) to an initial, and removes email addresses and phone numbers. This happens for every customer and can’t be turned off. Your originals stay in your instance.

  • Names and addresses on your screen. For the people Bervo knows about, the AI only sees a last initial ("Timothy E"), and it never sees email addresses or phone numbers. In Settings you can choose to see full last names and email addresses on your own screen. Both are off to start. You can turn them on for each of your assistants too, but never beyond what you see yourself. Bervo’s texts to your phone and anything the Bervo team sees stay masked.
  • Attachments. Bervo doesn’t download or save email attachments. If you ask about a specific attachment, Bervo reads that one file to answer you, with names and addresses masked as above, and then discards it (§3). For privacy reasons, Bervo cannot read scanned documents at this time. Bervo does not open pictures. Files you attach yourself in a chat with Bervo are different: the AI reads them as you give them (names aren’t masked), and they are kept with that conversation until you delete it.
  • Text messages. Photos, videos, and other attachments in your texts are never copied; a photo appears only as "[Photo shared]". You can exclude a contact: your instance deletes their texts the moment they arrive, before anything reads them. We encourage including everyone, because family threads often carry the plans and reminders Bervo is best at.

6. People you add to your instance

You may invite people to work in your instance with you, such as an assistant or a colleague. For each person we collect their name, email address, and login, and we record which of them did what, so actions in your instance are attributed to the person who took them. People you add can see the data in your instance; they are users of your instance, not sources of data, and Bervo never reads their own email, calendar, or messages. You are responsible for adding only people who are permitted to see the data you have connected, including under any obligation you have to your employer or clients, and for removing them when that permission ends. To remove a person, contact us and we end their access immediately; it takes effect on their very next request.

8. Microsoft account data

When you connect a Microsoft account, we request delegated permissions to read mail, calendars, and contacts. We request no permission to write or send mail. Your organization's administrator may need to approve Bervo for your tenant; that approval covers only the permissions listed on the consent screen.

9. AI processing

Summaries and answers are produced by sending the relevant text to a large-language-model provider (Anthropic) over an API. Where you supply your own API key, those requests are made under your account with that provider and are governed by its terms. Where Bervo supplies the key during a trial, requests are made under Bervo's account and the provider's API terms, which do not permit training on API inputs. We apply your privacy choices (§5) in your instance before anything is sent to the provider: text exclusions and name and address hiding. Search indexing uses a model that runs on the same server as your instance, so it sends nothing to the provider. Nothing excluded is sent to the provider.

What learns, and what doesn't. Your Bervo instance learns from your data and your corrections: how you write, who matters to you, what you want to be told about, and what you've committed to. That learning stays in your instance, is never used for another customer, and is destroyed when you delete your instance. The AI model provider does not learn from you: requests are sent under API terms that do not permit training on them, and no data of yours is used to train any model, by us or by anyone.

Voice (optional, off unless you turn it on). If you turn on Voice in Settings, what you say in Bervo's voice mode, and voice memos you send to Bervo's text line, are sent to Deepgram, Inc. to be turned into text. If you also use Bervo's natural voice, Bervo's spoken answers, which can include details from your email, texts, calendar or attachments, are sent to Deepgram to be read aloud; if you choose your phone's own voice instead, answers are read aloud by your phone and are not sent to Deepgram. We ask Deepgram not to use this audio or text to improve its models, and Deepgram keeps it only as long as it takes to process it. Bervo does not keep the audio: it is deleted as soon as it has been turned into text or played. The text of the conversation is kept in your chat like typed messages. Each person turns Voice on separately, and turning it off takes effect immediately.

10. Questions during setup

Setup screens have a Questions box. What you type there goes to the Bervo team, and a person replies. It is kept with your other feedback (§11).

Setup help by text. Until your own AI key is saved, Bervo answers your texts during setup using Bervo’s own AI account with Anthropic, not yours. Only your setup questions and your setup progress (which steps are done and which kinds of accounts you connected) are sent. Your email, texts, calendar, contacts and tasks never are. Names are hidden as described in §5. We keep your setup questions and Bervo’s answers to improve setup, and questions Bervo can’t answer go to the Bervo team (§11). Once your key is saved, Bervo uses your own account.

11. Beta feedback and the Beta Conversation Improvement Program

Feedback you send. During the beta you can send feedback to the Bervo team in three ways: by text (just tell Bervo something is wrong, confusing, or could be better), with the Feedback button, or by ticking "Also send to the Bervo team" on a comment. Feedback you write to us yourself (the Feedback button, or the Questions box during setup) reaches the Bervo team as you wrote it, with your name and address hiding choices (§5) applied, plus basic context (which screen, how many messages) but never your conversations. When you tell Bervo something went wrong by text, in chat, or in a Tell Bervo note, Bervo first decides whether it is about how you work or something to fix in Bervo for everyone. If it is for everyone, Bervo sends the team only a general description of the problem (which feature, what went wrong), never the content of your messages, data, or tasks, and tells you it has done so; the details stay in your instance. Each report is labeled with the account it came from, so the Bervo team can follow up with you about it or let you know when it’s fixed. If Bervo can’t tell, the Bervo team may be shown that same general description to decide, never your note itself. The Bervo team keeps it outside your instance, on Bervo's message service, until you ask us to delete it, and may be alerted to a problem report by text. If the Bervo team texts you asking for more detail about a problem you reported, what you send back reaches the team as you wrote it, except that people’s names are shortened (for example “Jane D”) and phone numbers and email addresses are removed. The question always tells you your reply goes to the Bervo team.

What you tell Bervo about how you work (for example, that a calendar block marks time booked elsewhere) is kept as an instruction in your own instance. It is used only for you. You can see, edit, or remove each one on the Learning page under Tell Bervo.

Beta Conversation Improvement Program (only with your consent). An opt-in beta feedback program that helps us improve how Bervo handles your chats and texts with it. If you join, an automatic nightly check runs in your own instance, using your own AI key, and reviews your conversations with Bervo (your chats and texts with Bervo only, not your email or your texts with other people) to find problems. It sends the Bervo team only a general description of each problem it finds (which feature, what went wrong), never the exchange itself or any of your content.

Joining is separate from agreeing to these terms. It is off unless you choose it, you can decline it and still use Bervo, and you can join or leave the program at any time by telling Bervo, by text or in the dashboard chat, or with the switch in Settings; a change applies from the next nightly check. You can also email privacy@bervo.ai and a team member will switch it off for you. It ends when the beta ends.

12. Retention and deletion

  • While you are a customer, your data is kept on your instance so the assistant can reason over history:
  • the full text of email you receive, and of texts you send and receive: a rolling 90 days;
  • mail you send: the full text for 90 days, then your own words (without the earlier messages quoted below them) for as long as your account exists;
  • summaries, and everything extracted from your messages (tasks, deadlines, commitments, relationships), plus your notes and conversations with Bervo: for as long as your account exists, however far back you chose to load;
  • a reply draft you save: until a reply from your mailbox appears in that conversation, or 90 days, whichever comes first.

Outside your instance, feedback and setup questions are kept as described in §11. - When you leave: during the private beta, deletion is by request: email privacy@bervo.ai, and a person handles it. We confirm the request is yours, then remove your instance within 2 days of your request (on Dedicated, by destroying your server; on Standard, by removing your account, its files and its backups from the shared server); remaining backup copies expire within 7 days after that, and we confirm completion in writing. What we hold about you outside your instance is deleted at the same time: your feedback and setup questions, and your phone number's link to Bervo's text line. Once paid plans exist, if your subscription ends without a deletion request, we do the same 30 days after it ends. - Your source data is never changed. Bervo only reads. Nothing Bervo does modifies or deletes anything in your email, calendar, contacts, or messages; they remain yours, at their source, before, during, and after you use Bervo. Deleting your Bervo instance deletes Bervo's copies and what Bervo generated, nothing else. - When you delete a message at its source. If you move a message to Trash, Deleted Items, Spam or Junk in a connected Gmail, Microsoft 365 or hosted Exchange mailbox, Bervo removes its copy at its next sync (usually within the hour) and closes any task it made only from that message. Copies in your instance's backups go when those backups expire. - Export. Everything Bervo generates for you — tasks, notes, initiatives, summaries, and your conversations with the assistant — is yours. Ask us and we will send you a copy of your instance's databases and your notes, and we offer it before any deletion. - Revocation without deletion: removing Bervo's access in your Google or Microsoft account, removing other email in Settings (which deletes its saved password) or deleting its app password with your provider, removing your Mac in Settings → Devices, or uninstalling the Mac app stops new data immediately; existing data remains on your instance until you ask us to delete it or you leave.

13. Security

The server your instance runs on has no open ports to the internet. Traffic reaches your instance only through an encrypted tunnel, after you enter a one-time code we email to your sign-in address, and your Bervo password sits on top of that. Credentials to your connected accounts are stored on your instance only, never in a shared store: Google and Microsoft accounts as revocable tokens, and a hosted Exchange mailbox or other email as its app or mailbox password, encrypted. Your AI key is stored the same way, on your instance only. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you without undue delay and within any period required by law.

14. Age and business use

Bervo is for business use by adults. We do not verify anyone's age. You are responsible for who you allow to use your instance.

15. Your rights

We honor access, export, and deletion requests from anyone, regardless of where you live, because your data sits in an instance of its own that we can copy for you or delete. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to correct or restrict your personal information, and to object to certain processing; we honor those too. Contact privacy@bervo.ai.
