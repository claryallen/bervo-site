Why Bervo · Assistants

How it works with your assistant.

Bervo is built around you. Your assistant works inside it with their own sign-in, and you decide what they can see and do.

Six steps, start to finish.

Step 1

Add your assistant

In Settings → Assistants, choose Add Assistant and enter their first name, last name and email. They get sign-in access automatically.

Step 2

They sign in as themselves

You get a one-time link to give your assistant. They choose their own password and sign in with their email. Nobody shares your sign-in.

Step 3

Choose what they can do

Switches for each person: see your text summaries, dismiss and complete items, set priority, and see your Daily Page.

Step 4

They work alongside you

Your assistant works your brief, tasks, email and calendar, marks items In Progress, Dismissed or Complete, and asks Bervo to create a task. They have their own Daily Page and their own private chats with Bervo.

Step 5

You see what they did

What they do on the dashboard is labeled with their first name. "Added by Assistant" shows the tasks they created, and the Daily Flow team log shows their actions by name, without their private chat text. Or ask Bervo, "What did Marcus do today?" You'll hear the tasks they finished, the emails they answered, the notes they added and what they wrote on their Daily Page.

Step 6

Remove access any time

Removing an assistant takes effect on their very next click. Their past work stays in the record, and you can restore access with one switch.

What the AI sees

Masked before anything reaches the AI.

Before anything from your email, calendar and texts reaches the AI, the last names of your contacts and the people you correspond with are truncated, and email addresses, phone numbers, and attachments are removed.

  • If masking ever fails, Bervo stops instead of sending.
  • Texts are optional. Connect them only if you choose.
  • Texts with anyone on your leave-out list are deleted the moment they arrive, before anything reads them.
How this compares
Where your data lives

Your own server, or your own walled-off account.

Your data is never pooled with other customers' data, and no database is shared with anyone else.

How this compares

Dedicated

Bervo runs on a private server that's yours alone.

Standard

Your Bervo runs in a walled-off account on a shared Bervo server. Your data, files and security keys stay apart from every other customer's.

No open doors

The server has no open ports to the internet. You reach it only through an encrypted tunnel, after a one-time code we email you, and then your Bervo password.

Your keys stay home

The connections to your accounts and your AI key are stored on your own server only, never in a shared store.

What Bervo can and can't do

It reads. It never sends.

Read-only

Bervo reads your mail, calendar and contacts. It cannot send, delete or change them.

Delete means gone

Delete an email in a connected mailbox and it leaves Bervo at the next sync. Archiving changes nothing.

Revoke anytime

Remove Bervo's access in your Google or Microsoft account, or remove the Mac app, and new data stops immediately.

How this compares

Who can see your data

You, and the people you choose.

The team never reads your content

Operator access is logged and limited. Feedback reaches the team as a general description, never your words.

Your assistant sees what you allow

You choose what your assistant can see and do, and you can remove their access instantly.

No selling, no advertising

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How this compares

The AI

Your AI, on your account.

Not used to train AI

Bervo doesn't train AI on your data, and under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use it for training by default. What Bervo learns about you is kept on your own server (in your own account on Standard); only feedback you choose to send reaches the Bervo team.

Your own AI account

The AI runs on your own account with Anthropic, so you see every cost, with an estimate of your first load during setup.

Your data, your exit

Take it with you, or have it deleted.

Export

Ask and we'll send you a copy of your databases and your notes. We offer it before any deletion.

Delete

We remove your server or account within 2 days of your request, remaining backups expire within 7 days, and we confirm in writing.

How long Bervo keeps things

The full text of mail you receive is kept for a rolling 90 days. Summaries, and the tasks and commitments drawn from your messages, stay for as long as you're a customer.
