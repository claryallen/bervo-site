Bervo

Bervo is a private AI executive assistant. It connects to your email, calendar, contacts and, if you choose, your texts, alongside your own tasks and notes. It keeps track of what's going on in your world so you can keep up, catch opportunities and prevent dropped balls. You get a daily briefing, a task list that keeps itself current and well-timed reminders. Use it on your dashboard, chat with it, or just text Bervo to ask a question, add a task or tell it something new. Your data lives on your own private server, closed to the internet, and you choose how names and contact details are masked before the AI sees anything. Bervo is in private beta.
